How to Order

Simple as 1–2–3

No complicated apps. Just a quick WhatsApp message and your dog eats well.

  1. 01

    Pick a recipe

    Browse our menu above and tap any available recipe card to see full ingredients, benefits, and details.

  2. 02

    Message us on WhatsApp

    Tap "Order Now" from inside any recipe or the button below. Tell us what you need — we'll confirm availability and pricing right away.

  3. 03

    Receive & serve fresh

    We'll arrange delivery or pickup. Keep it refrigerated at 0–4°C and serve your dog a meal made with real love.
